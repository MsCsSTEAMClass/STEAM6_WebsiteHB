Part 1: Problem Proposal

What problem are we solving, and what do we propose to do with it?

Before You Build:
Engineers don't begin by randomly building and hoping something works.
The first define the problem, investigate the system, and develop a plan.

Your team's Problem Proposal will document what you learned through research and your initial plan for addressing the problem.

Your proposal is NOT a final design. Engineers expect designs to change as they learn more, test ideas, and encounter problems.

Think of your proposal as a starting point, not a contract.




Part 2: Engineering Log

How did our design evolve as we built, tested, and improved it?

Now Build

As you build and test your system, your design will change. That's expected.
Engineers document those changes so they can understand what worked, what didn't, and why.


Engineering Log #1 - Build Check

Complete after your team has begun building and coding.


Engineering Log #2 - Test & Debug

Complete in the middle of your build, code, and debugging process.


Engineering Log #3 - Final Reflection

Complete BEFORE your group prepares the final presentation.
