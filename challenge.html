The Challenge


What am I making?

Your system must align with at least one of the following:

3.4.6-8.E-Collect, analyze and interpret environmental data to describe a local environment.

OR

3.4.6-8.H-Design a solution to an environmental issue in which individuals and societies can engage as stewards of the environment.


Possible Environmental Topics

You may choose your own idea, but here are some examples:

Ecosystems & Interactions

  • Plant response to sunlight
  • Animal response to seasonal temperatures
  • Pollinator activity and light levels
  • Predator-prey interactions

Human Impact

  • Air pollution alert system
  • Noise pollution monitoring
  • Deforestation detection
  • Urban heat island warning

Water Systems

  • Flood warning system
  • Drought response model
  • Water conservation monitor
  • River pollution detection


Climate & Weather

  • Storm alert model
  • Wildfire risk indicator
  • Temperature-driven habitat changes
  • Seasonal shift model

Stewardship & Solutions

  • Automated watering conservation system
  • Energy-efficient lighing model
  • Wildlife crossing alert system
  • Community recycling awareness device


Build Teams

You are working as a TEAM,
not just a group.

A TEAM:

  • Shares responsibility
  • Communicates respectfully
  • Divides tasks intentionally
  • Supports troubleshooting together

Engineering Log

  • One shared log per team
  • Each team member must complete
    at least 2 meaningful entries
  • Entries should include technical
    reasoning, (not just "We worked on it")


Once you choose your topic, you will continue to RESEARCH.
