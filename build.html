Project Requirements

Your project must clearly show:

  • One environmental issue
      • Come up with your own idea, or choose one from The Challenge page

  • One sensor
      • Examples:
    • Light
    • Dial
    • Sound
    • Temperature
    • Compass

  • One servo (Rotation / Position, your moving component)

  • Single-color LED


  • Tri-color LED

  • micro:bit display

  • Variable

  • Threshold

  • Physical Model
